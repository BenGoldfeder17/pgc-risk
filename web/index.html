pgc-risk

Estimated lifetime probability of 13 psychiatric disorders from a genotyping-chip file, using the Psychiatric Genomics Consortium's GWAS results. It runs in your browser. A statistical expectation, not a diagnosis.

Your genetic data is not uploaded. Your genome file is not sent to this site or to anyone else. Your browser opens it and reads it on your own device, and the results are worked out there. This page is locked so that its code cannot make network requests at all, so once it has loaded you can switch off your internet connection before choosing your file and it will still work.

How to read this

  • Your estimate is the lifetime risk among people whose polygenic score matches this one. It is not a prediction for one person: most people with a high score never develop the disorder, and most people who do develop it do not have a high score.
  • Common-variant scores capture about 7% of the liability to schizophrenia and bipolar disorder, 4% for depression and 2% or less for the others. Family history, life events and rare variants are not included.
  • Rows marked little information come from small studies: the estimate stays close to the baseline whatever the genotype.
  • The scores are calibrated for European ancestry only. Within Europe, regional ancestry can move a score by a few tenths of a standard deviation.
  • This is a research tool, not a medical test. If a result worries you, a doctor or a genetic counsellor is the right person to talk to.
